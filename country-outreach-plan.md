# Country Outreach Plan

Backlinks and mentions from sources based in a country carry geographic-relevance weight that a global link does not. This file lists **real, verified-to-exist** targets per market, in the order I would approach them.

**Two rules that apply to every line in this file:**

1. **No paid links, no link exchanges, no directory-submission services, no guest-post networks.** In a religious-education niche these get sites suppressed, and the sites that rank here have real community relationships instead.
2. **Everything here needs something to point at.** Right now the strongest asset you could offer — a named, credentialed teacher — does not exist on the site. Outreach before that lands will convert badly. Read the sequencing note at the end.

Every organisation named below was confirmed to exist at the time of writing. I have deliberately **not** invented contact names, email addresses or "warm intro" claims — you will need to find the right person at each one yourself, and the directories are mostly self-submission anyway.

## Tier 1 — United Kingdom

The strongest market for you: largest organised link surface, British English already native to the site, and the community-language match (Urdu, Bengali, Gujarati) is real.

| Target | What it is | Approach | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MuslimsInBritain.org** | The reference UK mosque directory, running since 2005, first-hand maintained | Not a listing surface for services — use it as your **research map** to find mosques by town, then approach them directly | Research |
| **mosquedirectory.co.uk** | 2,500+ UK mosque listings | Same: source list for direct mosque outreach | Research |
| **nearestmosque.com** | UK **and Ireland** mosque directory | Covers your Ireland page too | Research |
| **madrassah.co.uk** | Directory of UK Islamic educational institutes, searchable, with filters | **Check whether they accept online providers.** If they do, this is the single most relevant UK listing available to you | Listing |
| Individual mosques and Islamic centres | From the directories above | Offer free trial classes to the congregation; ask for a line on their "resources for families" page. Start with your own city and any mosque where someone actually knows you | Link + referral |
| University Islamic Societies | Most UK universities have one | Student rates. ISocs maintain resource pages and turn over every year, so the ask is low-stakes | Link |
| **Our Muslim Homeschool** (ourmuslimhomeschool.com) | Established UK Muslim homeschooling blog with an active readership | Editorial pitch only, from a **named** teacher. The pitchable topics are the safeguarding/vetting guide and "best age to start" — not your service | Link (Tier 2 timing) |
| **A Muslim Home School** (amuslimhomeschool.com) | Free printables and articles on Muslim children's education | Same — contribute something genuinely useful | Link (Tier 2 timing) |

**UK-specific credibility item:** *DBS check* is the term British parents look for on safeguarding. If your teachers have one, say so plainly on `/teachers`; if they don't, that is the highest-value operational thing you could fix for this market. Do not claim it either way until it is true.

| Target | What it is | Approach | Type |

That framing gets a warmer reception from the people who run them | Link + referral |
| **Ihsaan Home Academy** (ihsaanhomeacademy.com) | US-facing Muslim homeschooling resources, parenting reflections, book reviews | Editorial contribution from a named teacher | Link |

| **r/islam, r/converts, r/MuslimParenting** | Reddit | Answer questions properly, link only where it directly answers. One good answer beats fifty drops, and self-promotion posts will get you banned | Referral, no link equity |

|---|---|---|---|

| Toronto / Mississauga / Brampton mosque and Islamic centre sites | The densest Muslim population concentration in Canada | Direct outreach. This corridor alone is worth more than the rest of Canada combined | Link + referral |

## Tier 2 — Continental Europe

**An honest assessment before the table:** the link surface here is much thinner than the English-speaking markets, and it is harder for three reasons — most community sites are in the local language, there is no equivalent of MuslimsInBritain per country, and the large umbrella bodies are politically contested in ways that make them poor link targets regardless of SEO value (several have contested political associations that you do not want attached to a children's education brand). **Approach individual local mosques and community associations, not national umbrella organisations.**

| Country | Realistic targets | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Germany** | Local mosque and Islamic-centre sites in Berlin, Hamburg, Frankfurt, Munich, Cologne; Turkish community associations | Your **Turkish-speaking tutor** claim is the differentiator — verify it is true before leading with it. Most outreach will need to be in German or Turkish |
| **Netherlands** | Mosque sites in Amsterdam, Rotterdam, The Hague, Utrecht; Moroccan and Turkish community associations | Dutch Muslims search in both Dutch and English; the English page reaches the second group only |
| **France** | Local mosque sites in Paris, Marseille, Lyon; Arabic-first family networks | Hardest of the group without French-language content. Lowest priority for outreach effort |
| **Sweden / Norway / Denmark** | Local mosque and Islamic association sites in Stockholm, Malmö, Göteborg, Oslo, Copenhagen; **Somali community organisations** | The Somali-speaking tutor match is a real and under-served differentiator across all three. Nordic competitors group these countries together — so should your outreach |
| **Belgium** | Brussels and Antwerp mosque sites | Two language communities; pick one per approach rather than writing to both |
| **Spain / Italy** | Madrid, Barcelona, Milan, Rome community sites | Smallest organised surfaces. Treat as opportunistic, not a campaign |
| **Ireland** | `nearestmosque.com` covers Ireland; Dublin and Cork Islamic centres | Easiest of the non-UK European markets — English-speaking, small, under-served |

## The digital-PR asset that actually works across every market

One resource, useful to every Muslim parent in every country on this list, and not about you:

> **"How to check whether an online Quran teacher is actually qualified"** — what ijazah and sanad mean, what to ask in a trial class, what a real qualification chain looks like, what the red flags are, and what safeguarding questions to ask about one-to-one online classes with children.

Why this and not something else: it is the thing every parent on every one of these sites wants and nobody impartial has written well; it is linkable by mosques and homeschool groups *without* endorsing a commercial provider; and it is the natural pitch for every editorial target in Tier 2. The raw material is already on `/teachers` and in `/articles/choosing-an-online-quran-teacher`.

Localise the safeguarding section per market — **DBS** (UK), **Garda vetting** (Ireland), **police check / vulnerable sector check** (Canada), **WWCC** (Australia), state-level background checks (US). Those terms are what make it read as written for that country.

## Sequencing — do not start at the top of this file

1. **First: a named teacher on `/teachers`.** Every editorial pitch above needs a human byline. Outreach from "the teaching team" gets ignored, and a cold pitch you have already burned is hard to re-warm.
2. **Then: the vetting guide**, localised per market.
3. **Then: mosque and homeschool outreach** — the warm, local, referral-shaped approaches. Start in the city where someone already knows you.
4. **Then: editorial pitches** to the blogs, from the named teacher, with the guide as the offer.
5. **Reviews run in parallel from day one** — see `off-code-action-plan.md` §2. Ten real reviews will do more for your country pages than ten links.

## Realistic expectation

**Ten to fifteen genuine links across all fourteen countries in six months is a good outcome**, and most will come from the UK, US, Canada and Australia. Continental Europe may produce two or three in that time. Competitors have hundreds accumulated over a decade — you are not closing that gap, you are building enough corroboration to be a credible answer for specific country + course queries. That is a winnable goal; matching their link volume is not.
