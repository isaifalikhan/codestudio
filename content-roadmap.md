## Before the list: an honest read of the competitive field

The sites ranking for these terms — Riwaq Al Quran, AlQuranClasses, Buruj, Noor Academy, IQRA Network, eQuranCoaching, Shaykhi, Quran Schooling — are **dedicated domains, 5–12 years old, publishing weekly, with hundreds of blog posts each**. Several have full rule-by-rule Tajweed guides with a page per rule. This site is a subsection of a web-agency domain with 17 academy content pages.

You will not outrank them on head terms in 2026. What you *can* do, in this order:

1. **Win the questions they answer badly.** Most of these blog posts are thin, keyword-stuffed and contradictory (the "best age" pages alone claim 3, 5–7, and 5–10 without acknowledging each other). Genuinely useful, specific answers beat them for AI citation long before they beat them in blue links.
2. **Win long-tail and country-specific terms** — already built: 14 country pages, which most competitors don't have.
3. **Build head-term authority over 12–24 months**, if publishing stays consistent and real credentials arrive.

## Specification every piece must meet

1. **Direct answer first** — 2–3 plain sentences immediately after the H1, before any elaboration. This is what gets lifted into AI Overviews. Wrap in an element referenced by `speakable`.
2. **One H1**, logical H2s phrased as the questions people actually ask.
3. **900–1,400 words.** Below 800 reads as thin; above 1,600 only if the topic needs it.
4. **3–5 FAQs** with `FAQPage` schema.
5. **Internal links:** up down to its pillar (descriptive anchor text — "the hifz course", never "click here"), across to 2–3 sibling clusters, and out to the relevant course page.
6. **`Article` schema** with `datePublished`, `dateModified`, `wordCount`, `speakable`, and — once teachers are named — a real `author`.
7. **Religious content must be reviewed by a qualified teacher before publishing.** This is non-negotiable for anything touching rulings, Tajweed obligations or fiqh.
8. **No fabricated statistics.** If you can't cite it, don't claim it.

The existing 8 articles already meet this spec and can be used as templates — see `lib/academy-articles/`.

## How to add a piece

Content is structured data, not markdown blobs, so a new article is one file plus one line:

1. Add an `Article` object in `lib/academy-articles/<group>.ts` (fields: `slug`, `title`, `seoTitle?`, `description`, `excerpt`, `category`, `readingMinutes`, `published`, `updated`, `intro`, `takeaways`, `sections`, `faqs`, `relatedCourses`, `keywords`).
2. Register it in `lib/academy-articles/index.ts`.

Route, schema, sitemap entry, RSS item, `llms.txt` entry and internal links generate themselves.
